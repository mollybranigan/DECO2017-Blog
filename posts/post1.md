# Escaping Winter
On January 11th, 2026 I left Boston MA. After a long travel day and significant time change I landed in 
Sydney, Australia. I have spent my whole life in the United States, never living for than a 5 hour drive from 
where I was born so deciding to spend a semester is Australia was a striking difference. 

# Why Sydney?
I had visited Australia with my family two year before and fallen in love with the country. I love the outdoors so the
available parks and beaches attracted me. Fresh food and friendly people also helped. The shock about how far I am, 
both physically and time difference-wise, from family and friends will never feel normal though. 

# Who am I?
Follow along my blog to learn more about Adjusting to Sydney, travels, adventures, and my biggest discoveries. 
I also love to read so may throw in book suggestions or podcasts occasionally. o far I have been to New Zealand, 
Melbourne (for the Australian Open), Tasmania, Port Douglas (Cairns), and Bali but I am hoping to travel more. I 
really love Sydney and don't normally want to leave it which makes me travel less.
